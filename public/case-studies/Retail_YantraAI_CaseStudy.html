Market Context & Roadmap

Why Now? Urgency & Outlook

India's retail sector is at an inflection point — Tier-2 city expansion, rising shrinkage, and unmonitored CCTV footage create an urgent case for Vision AI adoption.

⚠️

India's Retail Surge Is Outpacing Manual Security & Operations

India has 750+ organised malls and thousands of standalone retail chains, with rapid expansion into Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities. Retail shrinkage remains at 1.5–2% of revenue — largely because 80%+ of CCTV footage is never reviewed. Queue abandonment drives 15–25% peak-hour revenue leakage. Retailers that deploy Vision AI infrastructure now gain a decisive competitive edge: lower shrinkage, higher conversion, and operational intelligence that manual teams simply cannot deliver.

Phase 1 — Store Pilot 1–2 stores. Core: footfall counting + loss prevention AI. Duration: 2–3 months.
Phase 2 — Chain Rollout All locations. Add: queue intelligence + shelf analytics + customer path tracking. Duration: 3–9 months.
Phase 3 — Retail Network Intelligence Cross-store benchmarking, HQ analytics dashboard, predictive replenishment signals. Ongoing.
